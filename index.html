Process

Shaped and fired

Every piece is shaped by hand from a high-quality clay, then fired in an oven so it sets hard.

Hand-painted

Eyes, teeth, and linework are painted on by hand, one piece at a time.

Finished in gloss

A gloss coat goes over the paint for a smooth, shiny finish.

Custom pieces

Have an idea for a one-of-a-kind piece? Send the details and I’ll reply by email with what’s possible and a price. Some finished custom pieces get shared on our TikTok.
