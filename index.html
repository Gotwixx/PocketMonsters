How it’s made

Shaped by hand

Every piece starts as air-dry clay, shaped by hand and never poured into a mold, so each one has its own quirks.

Painted in detail

Eyes, teeth, and spikes are painted on one piece at a time.

Sealed to last

A glossy sealer coat protects the paint and gives it a smooth finish.
